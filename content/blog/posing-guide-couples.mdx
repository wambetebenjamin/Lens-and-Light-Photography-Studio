Almost every couple tells us the same thing before a session: "just so you
know, we're not photogenic." Within ten minutes, they always are. Here's
what actually works.

## Give hands a job

Idle hands look stiff in every photo. We ask couples to hold hands, adjust
a collar, or hold a jacket over one shoulder — anything that gives hands
purpose.

## Walk, don't pose

Some of our favourite frames come from simply asking a couple to walk
toward us, then away, then stop and turn. Movement reads as candid even
when it's directed.

## Whisper something only they'd laugh at

We ask one partner to whisper an inside joke to the other. The real laugh
that follows is worth more than ten "say cheese" frames.

## Close the gap, then close it again

Most couples stand further apart than they think. We gently close the
physical distance between them twice during a session — it reads as
intimacy on camera, not distance.

## Shoot the in-between moments

The best shot is rarely the one right after "ready, 1, 2, 3." We keep
shooting for two or three extra seconds after every prompt, when
shoulders drop and real expressions return.
