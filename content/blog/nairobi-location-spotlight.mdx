Clients often ask where to shoot. Here are six Nairobi locations that give
us reliably beautiful frames in every season.

## 1. Karura Forest

Dappled light through indigenous forest canopy, bamboo groves and the
Karura waterfall make this our most-requested location for couples and
family sessions.

## 2. Nairobi Railway Museum

Weathered carriages and warm, textured walls give portraits an editorial,
timeless feel — especially beautiful in the late afternoon.

## 3. Karen Blixen Coffee Garden

Manicured gardens and soft natural light make this a favourite for
engagement sessions and bridal portraits.

## 4. The Nairobi Arboretum

Tall, mixed forest and open lawns within minutes of the CBD — ideal when
you want greenery without leaving the city.

## 5. Two Rivers Mall Waterfront

Clean architectural lines and reflective water make this a strong choice
for brand and corporate portrait sessions that want a modern backdrop.

## 6. Our Kilimani Studio

When the Nairobi weather won't cooperate, our studio gives us full control
over light, backdrop colour and temperature — any day, any time.

Not sure which location fits your session? Tell us the vibe you're going
for in your booking notes and we'll recommend the right spot.
